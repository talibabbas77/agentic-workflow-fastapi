# Pricing and billing

Parcelwise charges a monthly plan fee plus a small platform fee for each label. Postage is charged separately at the carrier's rate. This page explains the plans, how you pay for postage, other fees, and how billing works.

## Plans

| Plan | Monthly fee | Platform fee per label | Dashboard users included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $0 | $0.08 | 2 |
| Growth | $79 | $0.04 | 10 |
| Scale | $299 | $0.02 | 25 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited |

The platform fee is added to the postage for every label you buy. When a label's postage is refunded after a void, its platform fee is refunded too.

Growth and Scale can also be paid annually. Annual billing costs the same as 10 months of the monthly fee, so you get 12 months for the price of 10.

## Postage and your balance

Postage is paid from a prepaid balance in your Parcelwise account.

- **Add funds**: go to **Dashboard > Billing > Balance** and select **Add funds**. The minimum top-up is $25.
- **Auto-reload**: turn on auto-reload to top up automatically when your balance runs low. By default, we add $200 when the balance drops below $50. You can change both amounts.
- **Payment methods**: you can fund your balance with a card or by bank debit. Card top-ups have a 2.9 percent processing fee. Bank debit has no fee but takes 3 business days to clear, so the funds are not available right away.

Your plan fee and platform fees are charged to your card on file, not to your balance.

## Tracking and insurance fees

- Trackers for labels you buy through Parcelwise are free.
- Trackers for labels you bought elsewhere cost $0.02 each.

Shipping insurance covers loss and damage. It costs $0.90 for every $100 of declared value, with a minimum of $0.90 per parcel, and covers up to $5,000 per parcel. To add insurance, set `insurance_amount` when you buy the label. To make a claim, go to **Dashboard > Insurance > File claim** within 60 days of the ship date.

## Carrier adjustments

Carriers sometimes measure or weigh a parcel again after pickup. If the measured weight, dimensions, or service level differs from what you sent, the carrier bills an adjustment. Adjustments usually arrive 2 to 8 weeks after the ship date.

Adjustments are deducted from your balance and listed under **Dashboard > Billing > Adjustments**, along with the measurements the carrier recorded.

If you think an adjustment is wrong, select **Dispute** next to it within 30 days of the adjustment date. Attach photos of the packed parcel on a scale and next to a tape measure. Most disputes are answered by the carrier within 10 business days.

## Invoices and statements

- On the 1st of each month, we send an invoice for your plan fee and the platform fees for the previous month.
- Each balance top-up gets its own receipt.
- Download invoices and receipts under **Dashboard > Billing > Invoices**.
- For a line-by-line record of every label, select **Dashboard > Billing > Statements > Export** to download a CSV file.

To add a tax ID or change the billing address shown on invoices, go to **Dashboard > Billing > Details**. Changes apply to future invoices.

## Changing or canceling your plan

Change your plan under **Dashboard > Settings > Plan**.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately, and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the month.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing month.
- **Canceling** a paid plan moves your account to Starter at the end of the billing month. Starter has no monthly fee, so your account and history stay available.

To get unused balance back, select **Dashboard > Billing > Balance > Withdraw**. Withdrawals are available for balances over $10, are sent to the original payment method, and are processed within 10 business days. You must withdraw or use up your balance before you can close your account.
