# Plans and pricing

Lumen Notes has four plans: Free, Plus, Team, and Enterprise. This page explains what each plan costs and what it includes. For payment methods, invoices, and refunds, see Billing and refunds.

## Plan overview

| | Free | Plus | Team | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Price, billed monthly | $0 | $8 per month | $14 per seat per month | Custom quote |
| Price, billed annually | $0 | $72 per year | $144 per seat per year | Custom quote |
| Members | 1 | 1 | 3 to 500 seats | 50 seats or more |
| Best for | Trying Lumen | Individuals who write a lot | Teams | Larger organizations |

Prices are in US dollars and do not include tax. Billed annually, Plus works out to $6 per month and Team to $12 per seat per month.

Plus is a single-user plan. If you want to share a workspace with other members, you need Team or Enterprise.

## What the Free plan includes

The Free plan is meant for personal use and has no time limit. It includes:

- Unlimited notes and notebooks in your personal workspace.
- Sync across up to 2 devices at the same time. The web app does not count toward this limit, so you can always sign in from a browser.
- Joining team workspaces that other people invite you to. Your access there follows their plan, not yours.

If you sign in on a third device, you are asked to sign out of one of the other two first.

## Storage and file size limits

| Plan | Total storage | Largest single file |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 2 GB | 10 MB |
| Plus | 50 GB | 200 MB |
| Team | 100 GB per seat, pooled | 500 MB |
| Enterprise | Unlimited | 2 GB |

On Team, storage is pooled across the workspace. For example, a 10-seat workspace has 1,000 GB to share among all members, and one person can use more than 100 GB as long as the workspace total stays under the limit.

Storage counts attachments, images, and clipped pages. The text of your notes does not count toward storage. To see current usage, go to **Settings > Workspace > Usage**.

## Version history by plan

Lumen keeps earlier versions of every note so you can go back to them. How far back you can go depends on the plan:

| Plan | Version history |
|---|---|
| Free | 7 days |
| Plus | 90 days |
| Team | 1 year |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

To restore an earlier version, open the note and select **... > Version history**, pick a version from the list, and select **Restore this version**. Restoring creates a new version, so you can undo a restore the same way.

## Seats and guests

On Team and Enterprise, a seat is one member of the workspace. You are billed for every member, including admins, whether or not they sign in that month.

- When you add a member in the middle of a billing period, you are charged a prorated amount for the rest of that period on your next invoice.
- When you remove a member, the seat count goes down at your next renewal. There is no credit or refund for the unused part of the current period.

Guests are people outside your workspace who can open specific notebooks you share with them. Guests are free and do not use a seat. Guest limits by plan:

- Free: guests are not available.
- Plus: up to 5 guests.
- Team: up to 2 guests for each paid seat.
- Enterprise: unlimited guests.

## Discounts for nonprofits and education

- **Nonprofits**: 50 percent off the Team plan, on annual billing only. Apply at lumen-notes.example/nonprofit and attach your registration documents. Review takes up to 10 business days.
- **Students and teachers**: the Plus plan is free for 1 year when you verify a school email address. You can renew the discount each year by verifying again.

Discounts cannot be combined with each other or with promotional codes.

## Changing your plan

Go to **Settings > Billing > Change plan** to upgrade, downgrade, or switch between monthly and annual billing.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You are charged a prorated amount for the rest of the current billing period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period. You keep the features of your current plan until then.
- **Monthly to annual**: takes effect immediately, and the unused part of your current month is credited toward the annual price.

If you downgrade and your workspace uses more storage than the new plan allows, nothing is deleted. Existing files stay available, but you cannot upload new files until you are under the limit.
