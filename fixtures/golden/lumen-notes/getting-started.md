# Getting started with Lumen Notes

Lumen Notes is a note-taking and team knowledge app. You can write personal notes, organize them into notebooks, and share notebooks with the people you work with. This guide covers creating an account, installing the apps, and finding your way around for the first time.

## Create your account

1. Go to app.lumen-notes.example/signup.
2. Enter your email address and choose a password.
3. Open the confirmation email from noreply@lumen-notes.example and select **Confirm email**.

The confirmation link expires after 48 hours. If it expires, sign in with your email and password and select **Resend confirmation** in the yellow banner at the top of the screen.

Until you confirm your email address, you can create and edit notes, but you cannot invite people, share notes, or publish a public link.

Every new account starts on the Free plan. You do not need a payment method to sign up, and there is no trial that converts to a paid plan automatically.

## Install the desktop and mobile apps

You can use Lumen Notes in a web browser, but the desktop and mobile apps add offline access and system notifications. Download them from lumen-notes.example/download.

| App | Minimum supported version |
|---|---|
| Mac desktop | macOS 12 or later |
| Windows desktop | Windows 10 (64-bit) or later |
| Linux desktop | 64-bit distributions with glibc 2.31 or later (.deb and AppImage) |
| iPhone and iPad | iOS and iPadOS 16 or later |
| Android phones and tablets | Android 10 or later |

The web app supports the current and previous major version of the common desktop browsers. Mobile web browsers are not supported. On a phone or tablet, use the mobile app instead.

Desktop apps update themselves in the background. You can check your version under **Help > About Lumen Notes**.

## How your content is organized

Lumen Notes uses three levels:

- **Workspace**: the top-level container. Your account comes with one personal workspace, and you can join any number of team workspaces.
- **Notebook**: a collection of notes inside a workspace. Most people create one notebook per project, team, or topic.
- **Note**: a single page. A note can contain subpages, nested up to 5 levels deep.

Use the workspace switcher in the top-left corner to move between workspaces. Each workspace has its own members, billing, and settings, so a note in your personal workspace is never visible to a team workspace unless you move it there.

## Write your first note

1. Select **New note** in the sidebar, or press Ctrl+N (Cmd+N on Mac).
2. Type a title, then press Enter to start the body.
3. Type `/` on an empty line to open the block menu. From there you can add headings, checklists, tables, code blocks, images, and embeds.
4. To start from a template instead, select **Templates** in the empty note and pick one, such as Meeting notes or Project brief.

Notes save automatically as you type. There is no save button.

To tag a note, type `#` followed by the tag name anywhere in the body, for example `#roadmap`. Tags appear in the sidebar under **Tags** and are shared across all notebooks in the same workspace.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Action | Windows and Linux | Mac |
|---|---|---|
| Quick search | Ctrl+K | Cmd+K |
| New note | Ctrl+N | Cmd+N |
| Open today's daily note | Ctrl+Shift+D | Cmd+Shift+D |
| Toggle sidebar | Ctrl+\ | Cmd+\ |
| Show all shortcuts | Ctrl+/ | Cmd+/ |

You can change shortcuts in the desktop apps under **Settings > Preferences > Keyboard**. Shortcuts cannot be customized in the web app or the mobile apps.

## Getting help

If you cannot find an answer in the help center, contact the support team. How you reach us and how quickly we reply depends on your plan:

| Plan | Channels | First response target |
|---|---|---|
| Free | Community forum and email | 3 business days |
| Plus | Email | 1 business day |
| Team | Email and in-app chat | 8 business hours |
| Enterprise | Email, in-app chat, and a named support contact | 4 business hours |

Email support at support@lumen-notes.example. To open in-app chat, select the **?** icon in the bottom-left corner and choose **Chat with us**.

Support hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 20:00 UTC. Messages sent outside those hours are answered on the next business day, and the response target starts counting from then.
