# Billing and refunds

This page covers how Lumen Notes charges you, where to find invoices, what happens when a payment fails, and when you can get a refund. For plan prices and limits, see Plans and pricing.

## Who can manage billing

Only the workspace owner can update payment methods, change the plan, view invoices in the app, or cancel the subscription.

The owner can add up to 3 billing contacts under **Settings > Billing > Billing contacts**. Billing contacts receive copies of invoices and payment failure emails, but they cannot change anything. A billing contact does not need to be a member of the workspace, so you can add a finance team address.

If the owner has left your organization, ask an admin to contact support@lumen-notes.example to start an ownership review.

## Payment methods

Lumen Notes accepts:

- Credit and debit cards from the major card networks.
- Payment by invoice and bank transfer, for Enterprise plans and for annual Team subscriptions of $5,000 or more. Invoice payment terms are net 30.

We do not accept checks, cryptocurrency, or prepaid gift cards.

To update your card, go to **Settings > Billing > Payment method > Replace card**. The new card is used for the next charge. If you have an overdue invoice, open it and select **Pay now** to charge the new card right away.

## Invoices and receipts

You are charged on the same calendar day each billing period as your original purchase. If that day does not exist in a given month, for example the 31st, you are charged on the last day of that month.

After each charge, an invoice is emailed to the owner and to any billing contacts. You can also download every invoice as a PDF from **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.

To add a company name, billing address, or VAT or GST number, go to **Settings > Billing > Billing details**. Changes apply to future invoices only. To have a past invoice reissued with updated details, email billing@lumen-notes.example within 90 days of the invoice date.

## Taxes

Sales tax, VAT, or GST is added based on your billing address and shown as a separate line on each invoice.

- Businesses in regions that use the reverse charge mechanism can enter a valid VAT number in billing details. Tax is then not charged on future invoices.
- Tax-exempt organizations can email an exemption certificate to billing@lumen-notes.example. The exemption applies from the next invoice and is not applied to past invoices.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails, here is what happens:

1. We email the owner and billing contacts and retry the charge 3 more times: 3 days, 5 days, and 7 days after the first attempt.
2. If the last retry fails, the workspace becomes read-only. Members can still open and export notes, but nobody can create or edit content.
3. If the balance is still unpaid 30 days after the first failed attempt, the workspace moves to the Free plan. Your notes and files are kept, but anything above the Free plan limits stays read-only.

To fix a failed payment at any point, update your payment method. We charge the outstanding balance immediately and restore full access within a few minutes.

## Refunds

- **Monthly plans**: we do not refund partial months. When you cancel, you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.
- **Annual plans**: you get a full refund if you cancel within 30 days of the initial purchase or of an annual renewal. After 30 days, annual plans are not refundable, and you keep access until the end of the term.

Refunds go back to the original payment method and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

To request a refund, go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan** and choose **Cancel and request refund**. This option only appears when your subscription is eligible. You can also email billing@lumen-notes.example with your workspace name and invoice number.

## Canceling your subscription

1. Go to **Settings > Billing > Cancel plan**.
2. Optionally, tell us why you are leaving.
3. Select **Confirm cancellation**.

After you cancel, your paid features stay active until the end of the current billing period. Then the workspace moves to the Free plan. Canceling does not delete any notes.

To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date. If you change your mind before the period ends, go to **Settings > Billing** and select **Resume plan**. Your renewal date stays the same.
