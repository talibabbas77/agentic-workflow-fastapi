# Plans and billing

## Plans

| Plan | Price | Invoices per month | Team members |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 5 | 1 |
| Solo | $9 per month | Unlimited | 1 |
| Studio | $24 per month | Unlimited | Up to 5 |

All plans include card payments, reminders, and the tax report. Recurring invoices and multiple currencies need Solo or Studio.

## Change your plan

Upgrade or downgrade in Settings > Plan. Upgrades start right away and you pay the difference for the rest of the month. Downgrades take effect at your next renewal date.

## Refunds for your Tallyfox subscription

If you are not happy, write to us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. After that, you can cancel at any time and keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for. We do not refund partial months.

## Your Tallyfox invoices

Your own Tallyfox invoices are in Settings > Billing history. You can add a company name, address, and VAT number there, and they appear on every new invoice.
