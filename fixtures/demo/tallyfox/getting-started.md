# Getting started with Tallyfox

Tallyfox is invoicing for freelancers and small studios. You create invoices, send them by email, and get paid by card or bank transfer.

## Create your first invoice

Open Invoices and choose New invoice. Pick a client, add line items with a description, quantity, and price, and set a due date. Tallyfox fills in your business details from Settings > Business profile. Choose Send to email the invoice as a PDF with a link your client can pay from.

## Add and manage clients

Add a client from Clients > New client, or type a new name while creating an invoice. Each client keeps a billing email, an address, a default currency, and default payment terms. Archived clients keep their invoice history but no longer show in pickers.

## Invoice numbers

Invoices are numbered in order, starting at INV-0001. You can change the prefix and the next number in Settings > Invoices. Numbers never repeat, even if you delete a draft, because many tax offices require an unbroken sequence.

## Recurring invoices

For retainers, open an invoice and choose Make recurring. Pick weekly, monthly, or quarterly, and Tallyfox creates and sends a copy on that schedule. Recurring invoices stop automatically after an end date if you set one.
