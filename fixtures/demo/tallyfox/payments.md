# Getting paid

## Accept card payments

Connect a Stripe account in Settings > Payments to let clients pay invoices by card. Card payments arrive in your Stripe balance, and Tallyfox marks the invoice as paid automatically. Tallyfox adds no fee on top of Stripe's processing fee.

## Bank transfer

Add your bank details in Settings > Payments to print them on every invoice. Bank transfers are not detected automatically, so mark the invoice as paid when the money arrives.

## Payment reminders

Turn on reminders in Settings > Reminders. Tallyfox emails the client 3 days before the due date, on the due date, and 7 days after. You can change the schedule or turn reminders off for a single invoice from its menu.

## Mark an invoice as paid or partly paid

Open the invoice and choose Record payment. Enter the amount and date. If the amount is less than the total, the invoice shows as partly paid and the balance stays due. Reminders keep running until the balance is zero.

## Refund a client

Card payments can be refunded from the invoice: choose Refund and enter the amount. The refund goes back through Stripe and usually reaches the client's card in 5 to 10 business days. For bank transfers, send the money back yourself and record a negative payment.
