# Taxes and currencies

## Tax rates

Add tax rates in Settings > Taxes, such as VAT 20% or GST 10%. Pick a default rate, or set rates per line item. Tallyfox shows the tax total on the invoice and in the tax report. Tallyfox does not file tax returns for you.

## Tax report

Reports > Taxes lists the tax you charged per rate for any date range. Export it as CSV for your accountant.

## Bill in another currency

Each client can have its own currency. Invoices are sent in that currency, and Tallyfox converts totals to your home currency in reports using the exchange rate from the day the invoice was sent. Supported currencies are listed in Settings > Currencies.
